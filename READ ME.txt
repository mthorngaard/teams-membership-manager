TEAMS AND CHANNEL ADMINISTRATION

1. Copy the project to a local folder on the computer, and start it from that folder.
2. Install Node.js 22 or newer and PowerShell 7.
3. Double-click Start.cmd.
4. Open http://localhost:3000 and choose Sign in with Graph.
5. Sign in with your organization's work account.
6. Get teams and channels, and build your own access list.
7. Enter the user's sign-in address, select access, and confirm the addition.

The list starts empty. No organization data is included.
The Graph module is installed automatically when needed for the current user.
The account must have the required permissions in the organization.
Leave the start window open. Stop with Ctrl+C.
See README.md for the full guide and prerequisites.
